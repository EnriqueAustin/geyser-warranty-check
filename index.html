    How the Ariston serial works
    3680036Z2202651003658

    The highlighted group is the manufacture date: 20 is the year (2020) and 265 is the day of that year (21 September). The first group is the product reference and the last is a random number.

    The cylinder has 5 years' warranty from the manufacture date, or from the purchase or installation date if the client has proof. The same label format is used on Heat Tech and Ariston geysers. Quote all 21 characters when you log a call.

    How the Kwikot code works
    11-K-5

    11 is the month made (November). K is the year letter (2008). 5 is the tank warranty in years, counted from the manufacture month.

    O and Q aren't used. Each letter stands for two years, 24 years apart, so the tool picks the most recent one that isn't in the future. 2025 to 2030 (D to I) are confirmed by Kwikot; the rest follow the same sequence.
