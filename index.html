Before you call it a Kwikot claim

Tick anything that applies. Kwikot can refuse the claim for these.

A replacement geyser keeps the rest of the original warranty; it doesn't start again. On geysers older than a year, electrical parts carry a 2-month warranty.

Log the claim with Kwikot

  1. Leave the geyser in place. Kwikot or its agent must inspect it first.
  2. Isolate power and water and make it safe.
  3. Have the geyser code, serial number and model number from the plate.
  4. If the plate has faded: tax invoice, CoC or installer's proof of installation, plus the client's full details and address. Cash slips without customer details aren't accepted.
  5. Keep the reference number Kwikot gives you. It's the claim number for follow-ups. Turnaround is usually 24 business hours.

How the code works

11-K-5

11 is the month made (November). K is the year letter (2008). 5 is the tank warranty in years, counted from the manufacture month.

O and Q aren't used. Letters repeat every 24 years, so the tool picks the most recent year that isn't in the future. Highlighted letters are confirmed by Kwikot; the rest are calculated from them.
